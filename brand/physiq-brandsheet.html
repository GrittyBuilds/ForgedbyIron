Usage
✓ Do
Keep clear space ≥ the ring stroke around the mark. Bone/aqua logo on dark, ink/aqua-deep on light. Keep the ring gap open — it's the identity. Use aqua as the only accent, sparingly.
✕ Don't
Close the ring into a full circle (it starts to read like a search loupe), recolor the ring, add a second accent color, stretch or skew the mark, or put drop shadows on the wordmark.
