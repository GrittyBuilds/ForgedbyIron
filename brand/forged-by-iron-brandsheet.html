Usage
✓ Do
Keep clear space ≥ the plate-stack height around the mark. Bone logo on dark UI, ink logo on light. Scale the whole lockup proportionally. Keep the ember collar as the only warm accent.
✕ Don't
Recolor the F or the plates, stretch or skew the mark, add drop shadows to the wordmark, drop the plates off the top stroke, or place the bone logo on busy photos or low-contrast fills.
